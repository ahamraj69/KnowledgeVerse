# 📄 KnowledgeVerse Global User Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy defines exactly how KnowledgeVerse collects, manages, and purges user data points across production environments.

## 📊 1. DATA COLLECTION FACT SHEETS
*   **Account Profiles:** Standard name and email variables are written directly to secure fields.
*   **Cognitive AI History:** Prompt inquiries sent to the AI Tutor are logged strictly inside your user container to maintain conversation memory frames.
*   **Verification Credentials:** Government identification scans and teaching certificates are isolated in write-only storage folders, accessed only by verified administrators.

## 🛡️ 2. YOUR INDIVIDUAL PRIVACY RIGHTS
KnowledgeVerse respects international right-to-be-forgotten privacy controls. At any point, users can launch their Privacy Hub to trigger a complete cascade account data purge or export their entire data portfolio.
